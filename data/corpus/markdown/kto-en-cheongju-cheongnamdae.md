# Cheongnamdae Presidential Villa (청남대)

Address: 646 Cheongnamdae-gil, Sangdang-gu, Cheongju-si, Chungcheongbuk-do

Homepage: chnam.chungbuk.go.kr

Cheongnamdae, which means "Cheong Wa Dae in a warm southern land," was once a private villa used by former presidents of Korea. Cheongnamdae became open to the public on April 18, 2003. It is located near Daecheong Dam in Cheongwon-gu, Chungcheongbuk-do and was built in 1983. The area consists of a main building, golf range, heliport, and fishing area. The natural ecosystem is also well-preserved, providing a habitat for Natural Monument such as otters, flying squirrels, wild boars, water deer, and raccoons, and it serves as a migratory bird sanctuary.
