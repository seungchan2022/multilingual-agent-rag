# Cheonan Three-way Intersection Park (천안삼거리공원)

Address: 410 Chungjeol-ro, Dongnam-gu, Cheonan-si, Chungcheongnam-do

Homepage: www.cheonan.go.kr

The Cheonan Three-way Intersection Park was established in the 1970s to commemorate Cheonan Samgeori, a junction of the Samnamdaero (Great Road connecting Hanyang, Gyeongsang-do, and Jeolla-do) since the Joseon dynasty and famous for the folk song Heungtaryeong. The space, previously used primarily for events and parking, was redeveloped and reopened in September 2025 as a family-friendly theme park. The park is dividied into four themed areas, with a circular trail, forest trail, wildflower path, and waterside trail connecting the park, making it an ideal place for outings and walks where visitors can enjoy the Musical Fountain, Ground Fountain, Water Park, and mirror pond.
